Accessibility is not the removal of character. It is the removal of obstacles that prevent character from being understood.

Rhythm before ornament

Comfortable reading begins with rhythm. Line height gives the eye a reliable path back to the start of the next line. Paragraph spacing signals that one thought has ended and another is beginning.

  • Keep prose lines within a readable measure.
  • Use spacing to express hierarchy instead of adding visual noise.
  • Preserve native controls and interaction patterns.
  • Allow readers to choose the level of intervention they need.

A reversible layer

Applying a profile should not rewrite the lesson or disable existing behavior. Resetting it should return the page to its original state.

Soft eases visual load. Optimal balances pace and clarity. Deep compresses density for long-form focus sessions.
